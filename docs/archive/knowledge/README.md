# Archived M8 knowledge notes

The original knowledge notes and the old app's memecoin doctrine, kept for reference only.
They are not loaded into M8's prompt.
The audit and what was kept are in [decision 0016](../../decisions/0016-m8-knowledge.md); the result is [convex/m8/knowledge/coaching.md](../../../convex/m8/knowledge/coaching.md).

Why they are archived rather than used:

- They were written for the old app and call tools TrenchM8 doesn't have (`getPlaybooks`, `getJournalEntries`, `recordChartAnalysis` and others).
- Much of the advice is a buy, sell or hold call, or a verdict on a coin, which the doctrine rules out.
- They hold thresholds and past-trade figures as prose; in TrenchM8 numbers come from code.
- Loaded whole they are about 27K tokens, which costs more on every turn and dilutes the doctrine.
- The source guide and `review.md` they cite are not in this repo. We are allowed to use the content (confirmed 2026-10-08).
